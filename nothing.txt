  🟠 Important: fix before designing the database

  1. Mentor pins (ARC-001): the data model stores pin audience by group, but mentor posts are pinned for one section. It should be section_id.
  2. Missing tables (ARC-005): the PRD describes these features but has no table for them:
     - audit log
     - settings
     - block and mute
     - banned words
     - login history
     - AI usage
     - recording and material views
  3. Class meetings (ARC-006): courses are stored as a list inside one field. That needs its own table with a no-duplicates rule. The PRD also doesn't say what happens to future classes when the Admin changes the routine.
  4. Moving a student to another section (ARC-020): their old attendance and results history is lost.
  5. Enrollment (ARC-009): one part says enrollment is "only for optional courses", and another says students are enrolled in every course automatically.
  6. Class 11 → 12 (ARC-008): the student's class is stored in two places.
  7. Private details (ARC-018): §8 says teachers can see a student's mobile, address and guardian, but §5.3.1 says they can't.
  8. Attendance (ARC-019):
     - There's no holiday calendar.
     - It doesn't say whether a class with no attendance taken counts as "held".
     - It doesn't say what to show when the percentage divides by zero.
  9. Exams:
     - No scoring rule for questions with several correct answers, or for which quiz attempt counts (ARC-007).
     - Students who finish first can share the correct answers while others are still taking the exam (ARC-021).
     - It doesn't say whether a student who starts late gets cut off at the end of the exam window (ARC-022).
     - Editing a question in the question bank also changes old exams (ARC-022).
  10. Child safety (ARC-011): anyone can message minors one-to-one, and the Admin sees only chats that someone reports.
  11. AI chatbox (ARC-017): the agent found that the free Gemini API terms don't allow users under 18. Most HSC students are 16–18.

  🟡 Smaller problems

  - Phases (ARC-004): some features are listed in different phases in different sections, for example push notifications and notice emails.
  - Admin attendance (ARC-002): one place says "view only", another says "can edit".
